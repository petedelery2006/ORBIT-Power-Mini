# ORBIT Power Mini (O.P.M.)

The ORBIT Power Mini is a compact power bank project designed around a stated **1.2 Ah (1,200 mAh) capacity** and **two USB-C charging/output ports**.

## Project files

- `ORBIT Power mini 1.2ah.stl` — enclosure model
- `ORBIT Power mini 1.2ah(TPU case).stl` — flexible TPU case model
- `O.P.M parts list.md` — supplied parts list and component links

## Parts and battery configuration

See [O.P.M parts list](O.P.M%20parts%20list.md) for the original bill of materials. It lists three 2,000 mAh cells and three 2,100 mAh cells, alongside the charging module, 5 V boost board, switch, and USB-C ports. The relationship between those cell quantities and the stated 1.2 Ah capacity is not specified in the source list; confirm the intended cell configuration and electrical design before assembly.

## Status

This repository contains the supplied 3D models and parts list. Electrical schematics, wiring, charging behavior, port power limits, and build instructions have not been provided here.

## Safety

Lithium-polymer cells and charging circuits require suitable protection, correct wiring, and safe charging practices. Verify the selected cells, charge controller, boost converter, port wiring, and enclosure fit before building or charging a device. Do not infer supported USB-C current or power from the port count alone.
